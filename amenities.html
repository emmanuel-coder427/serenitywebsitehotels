Our Ammenities

Swimming pool

Dive into luxury with our stunning outdoor swimming pool. Surrounded by lush greenery, it's the perfect place to unwind and soak up the sun. Whether you're in the mood for a refreshing swim or lounging poolside with a cool drink, our pool area provides an idyllic retreat.

Spa and Wellness center

Treat yourself to a rejuvenating experience at our Spa and Wellness Center. Indulge in a variety of treatments, including massages, facials, and body therapies, designed to refresh your body and mind. With serene ambiance and professional therapists, you’ll feel completely restored.

24/7 Room Service

Experience convenience like never before with our round-the-clock room service. Whether it's breakfast in bed, a late-night snack, or a gourmet meal, our dedicated team is here to serve you anytime. You’re just a call away from enjoying delicious meals in the comfort of your room.

Laundry & Dry cleaning services

Our professional laundry and dry-cleaning services ensure you always look your best. No need to worry about your clothes while on vacation or business. Just let us know, and we'll take care of everything — your clothes will be fresh, clean, and ready for you to wear again.

Resturant and Dining

Savor mouthwatering meals at our on-site restaurant, where local flavors and international dishes are expertly prepared by our talented chefs. From a quick breakfast to a fine dining experience, we offer something to satisfy every craving. Don't miss our signature dishes, crafted with the freshest ingredients.

Conference and Event center

Host your next event at Serenity Hills Hotel in our fully-equipped conference and event center. Whether it’s a business meeting, a wedding, or a corporate retreat, we offer modern facilities, high-speed internet, and event planning services to ensure your event is a success.

Fitness center

Keep up with your fitness routine in our state-of-the-art fitness center. Equipped with the latest machines, free weights, and a dedicated space for yoga, it’s the ideal place to stay active while you’re away from home. Our gym is open 24/7, so you can work out on your schedule.
